Privacy & limitations

Beige Engine is an experimental, satirical interactive art experience, not a clinical service.

Camera

Camera use is optional. If enabled, your browser requests camera permission. Facial landmark analysis runs locally in the browser; this implementation does not send camera frames, recordings, or derived gaze signals to a Beige Engine server. Camera tracks are stopped at the end of the session or when you leave the page.

Third-party model downloads

Optional webcam mode loads MediaPipe software from jsDelivr and a model file from Google-hosted storage. Those services receive ordinary network requests, such as your IP address, according to their own policies. Without webcam mode these model requests are not made.

Local storage

A single best-streak duration is stored in your browser's local storage. No account is required. This implementation does not include site analytics or session uploads. Infrastructure-level access logs may be processed by the hosting provider.

What the score means

The score is a rough interaction proxy inferred from pointer movement or webcam relative eye-landmark and head movement, not a scientific measurement of emotional arousal, dopamine, boredom, attention, or health. It can be inaccurate, especially with glasses, lighting issues, or limited movement.

For questions about this experiment, please use the creator's public project contact channel. This privacy notice describes the supplied launch candidate; verify it against any third-party scripts added before publishing.
